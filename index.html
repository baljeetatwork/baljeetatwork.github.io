Featured · Capstone MBI806B · 2026 · in progress

Airline Delay Analytics — from raw CSV to decision dashboard

Problem. Which carriers and airports drive arrival delays, and can the monthly delay rate be predicted well enough to support scheduling decisions?

What I built. A Google Colab pipeline over 398,233 rows of US BTS data (2003–2025): cleaning and a star schema, EDA, feature engineering, and three regression models compared on RMSE, MAE and R², surfaced in a Power BI report.

Result. XGBoost performed best (R² 0.391, RMSE 0.0795) — a meaningful lift over the linear baseline (R² 0.154), with feature importance pointing to where operational attention should go.
